Common Questions

How much for a birthday party?

Private party packages start from £350 for a 1-hour set. Longer performances and full evening entertainment available. Get in touch with your date and I'll send you options.

Indoor and outdoor events?

Both. I've played in living rooms, gardens, marquees, hired venues, and everything in between. I bring my own portable equipment suitable for any setting.

How much space do you need?

Not much—about 2m x 2m. I'm fully mobile during performances too, so I can move through your guests if the space allows. We'll discuss setup when you book.

Can you learn a special song?

Absolutely. Anniversary songs, favourite tracks, meaningful tunes—let me know when you book and I'll make sure it's part of the set.
