Common Questions

What type of corporate events do you play?

Awards ceremonies, product launches, conferences, networking events, Christmas parties, gala dinners, team celebrations, and more. If you're hosting a corporate event, I can tailor my performance to suit.

Background music or headline performance?

Both. I can provide subtle ambient sets for networking and dining, or high-energy headline performances with DJ collaboration for celebrations and after-parties.

Do you have PLI insurance?

Yes. I carry full Public Liability Insurance with £10 million cover. All equipment is PAT tested. I can provide certificates on request for your venue or compliance team.

How far in advance should we book?

For major events, 2-3 months is ideal. Christmas party season books up quickly—September onwards I'd recommend booking early. Last-minute bookings sometimes possible.
