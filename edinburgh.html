Common Questions

How much does a saxophonist cost in Edinburgh?

Prices start from £350 for parties, £400+ for weddings. It depends on the event type, duration, and requirements. Get in touch with your date and I'll send you a quote.

Which Edinburgh venues have you played?

Most of them! Prestonfield, The Balmoral, Waldorf Astoria, Mansfield Traquair, Hopetoun House, The Hub, Dynamic Earth—I know Edinburgh's venues well.

Do you travel outside Edinburgh?

Yes. I regularly perform across Scotland and the UK. Travel is included within the Edinburgh area; other locations quoted individually. Glasgow, Fife, East Lothian—all regularly covered.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, as early as possible—popular dates book up quickly. For parties and events, 4-6 weeks is usually fine. Last-minute bookings sometimes possible.
