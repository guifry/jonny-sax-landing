Common Questions

Is there a travel fee for Glasgow?

No. Glasgow bookings include travel from Edinburgh at no extra charge. Same pricing as Edinburgh events.

Which Glasgow venues have you played?

Mar Hall, House for an Art Lover, The Grand Central, 29 Private Members Club, The Corinthian, Òran Mór, Blythswood Square—I know Glasgow's venues well.

Do you cover the wider Glasgow area?

Yes. Paisley, East Kilbride, Hamilton, Motherwell, Helensburgh, Loch Lomond—all regularly covered with no additional travel charge.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, as early as possible. For parties and events, 4-6 weeks is usually fine. I'm in Glasgow most weekends, so availability is generally good.
