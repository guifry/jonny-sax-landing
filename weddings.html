Common Questions

How much does a wedding saxophonist cost in Scotland?

Packages start from £400 for a single set. Full-day coverage (ceremony through evening party) is available. I'll send you my 2026 price list with all options when you enquire.

Can you learn a specific song for our ceremony?

Absolutely. I regularly learn special songs for couples—whether it's your first dance, a family favourite, or something meaningful to you. Just let me know when you book.

How long do you perform for?

Standard sets are 2 hours (with breaks). I can perform multiple sets throughout the day—ceremony, drinks reception, dinner, and evening—tailored to your schedule.

Do you travel outside Edinburgh?

Yes. I perform across Scotland—Glasgow, Aberdeen, St Andrews, the Highlands, and everywhere in between. Travel is included within central Scotland; other locations quoted individually.
