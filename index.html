About Jonny

Jonny Diggens is a contemporary pop and R&B saxophonist with over 13 years of experience performing at events around the world.

From performing after Jess Glynne at the launch of the TUI Discovery, to playing with multiple high-profile DJs, meeting and playing for Bob Reynolds (saxophonist for John Mayer and Snarky Puppy) in America, and performing for King Charles at a private garden party.

Known for bringing smooth sax lines and high-energy vibes, Jonny blends live sax perfectly with modern pop, R&B, and dance music. Ideal for parties, events, and special occasions where you want something memorable.
