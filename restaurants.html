Common Questions

Residency or one-off booking?

Both available. Residencies (weekly/fortnightly) build customer expectation and loyalty. One-off bookings work well for special occasions or trial runs.

What genres do you play?

Jazz standards, smooth jazz, soul, Motown, contemporary pop covers. I tailor the setlist to your venue's vibe—we'll discuss this when you get in touch.

What about volume levels for dining?

I play acoustically or with minimal amplification. Volume is always appropriate for the setting—background ambience that enhances conversation, not competes with it.

What equipment do you bring?

I'm self-contained. Saxophone, small PA if needed, and backing tracks for solo performances. No setup demands on your team.
